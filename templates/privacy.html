Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Spendly collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Data We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run the service:

  • Account information — your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Expense data — the amounts, categories, dates and descriptions you enter.
  • Basic technical data — such as browser type and log information used to keep the service secure and working.

We do not collect bank account details, card numbers or connect to your financial institutions.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your Spendly account.
  • Store your expenses and show you summaries and spending breakdowns.
  • Keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • Contact you about important changes to the service or these policies.

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal or financial information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database and is accessible only to you through your account. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your expenses or your account, the related data is removed from our active systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers, such as hosting services and web font providers, to operate the website. These providers may process limited technical data only as needed to deliver their services and are not permitted to use your information for any other purpose.

We do not share your expense data with third parties unless required by law.

5. Your Rights

You can view, edit or delete your expense data at any time from within the app. You may also request deletion of your account and all associated information.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please contact the Spendly team. For the rules that govern use of the service, see our Terms and Conditions.
